Employees must provide 90 days of written notice before terminating employment without cause. The notice must be submitted in writing to People Operations and becomes effective on the date People Operations confirms receipt. Notice periods run continuously and are not paused by holidays.

The 90-day notice period applies to all departments and all employee grades. Managers may not shorten or waive the notice requirement on behalf of the company. Extension agreements require written approval from the Chief People Officer.

This policy is effective January 15, 2026 and supersedes the previous notice period policy. Questions about the notice period should be directed to People Operations.
