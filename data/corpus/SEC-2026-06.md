Protected health information must never be sent by email. The encrypted portal is the only approved channel for sharing protected health information.

Mistakes involving protected health information, including misdirected email, must be reported to Compliance within 24 hours. Quick reporting limits harm to patients and the company.

Printed materials containing protected health information are stored in locked cabinets and shredded on disposal.
