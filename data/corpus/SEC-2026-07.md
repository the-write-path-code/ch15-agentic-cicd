Vendors that handle company or employee data must complete a security assessment before onboarding. The assessment covers access controls, encryption, and incident response.

Vendors are reassessed annually. Vendors with data access must sign a data processing agreement before receiving any data.

Vendor access is limited to the systems named in the agreement. Unused vendor accounts are disabled during the quarterly access review.
