Employees must provide 60 days of written notice before terminating employment without cause. The 60-day notice requirement applies to all departments. Notice must be delivered in writing to People Operations.

The notice period begins on the date People Operations receives the written notice. Employees who leave before the 60-day notice period ends may have final incentive payments adjusted, as described in the compensation guidelines.

This notice policy is effective March 1, 2024. HR-2024-11 is superseded by HR-2026-03, the current notice period policy. References to the 60-day notice requirement should be read against HR-2026-03.
