Company data is classified into three levels: public, internal, and restricted. Public data may be shared outside the company. Internal data stays within the company.

Restricted data is limited to named roles and requires encryption in transit and at rest. Handling rules for each level are described in the handling standard.

Every document carries its classification in the document header. Unclassified data is treated as internal by default.
