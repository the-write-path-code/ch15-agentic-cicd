Flights under six hours are booked in economy class. International flights longer than six hours may be booked in business class. Domestic premium cabins require director approval.

All travel must be booked through the travel desk at least 14 days before departure. Late bookings need a written justification attached to the expense report.

Ground transportation is reimbursed at cost with receipts. Rental cars are reserved through the travel desk under the company rate.
