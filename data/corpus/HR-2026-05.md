Field Operations employees must provide 45 days of written notice before terminating employment without cause. The shorter notice period reflects the seasonal scheduling of field crews and the cross-training time needed for replacements.

The 45-day notice exception applies only to Field Operations roles based at field sites. Field Operations employees at headquarters follow the standard notice period. The exception is granted under the current notice period policy.

Field Operations supervisors must confirm receipt of the written notice with People Operations within five business days.
