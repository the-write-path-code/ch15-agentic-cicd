Purchases under $10,000 require manager approval. Purchases between $10,000 and $25,000 require Director approval.

Capital expenditures above $25,000 require Vice President approval. Capital expenditures above $100,000 require Chief Financial Officer approval.

Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to stay under a threshold is a policy violation and is reviewed by Finance. All approvals must be recorded in the purchasing system.
