Employment records are retained for 7 years after separation. This includes contracts, performance reviews, and disciplinary records.

Payroll records are retained for 4 years. Benefits enrollment records are retained for 6 years after the plan year ends.

Records under legal hold are kept until the hold is released by Legal. Retention schedules are reviewed annually by the records office.
