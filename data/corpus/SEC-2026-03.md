Quarterly access reviews are owned by system owners. Each system owner confirms that active accounts match current role assignments.

Departing employees are deprovisioned within 24 hours of their last working day. Contractor accounts expire at the end of the contract term.

Privileged accounts are reviewed quarterly with documented sign-off. Findings are tracked to closure in the access management system.
