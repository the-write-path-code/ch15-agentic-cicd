Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid time off per month of service. Part-time employees accrue a proportional amount based on scheduled hours.

Unused paid time off carries over into the new year up to a cap of 5 days. Amounts above the cap expire at the end of the year.

Paid time off requests must be submitted at least 10 business days in advance and approved by the employee's manager. Emergency requests are reviewed case by case.
