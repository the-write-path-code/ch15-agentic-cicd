Department heads may approve remote work requests for their teams, up to three days per week. Requests must be made in writing and renewed each quarter.

Remote work arrangements do not change core hours or on-call expectations. Employees working remotely must remain reachable during business hours.

Remote work equipment is issued by the company and returned at the end of the arrangement. Residency and tax implications are reviewed by People Operations.
