Visitors must be escorted by a badge holder at all times in production and office areas. Visitor badges are issued at reception and collected at the end of the visit.

Lost or stolen badges must be reported within 4 hours. Replacement badges are issued after the report is filed.

Badge holders are responsible for entries made with their badge. Tailgating through controlled doors is a policy violation.
