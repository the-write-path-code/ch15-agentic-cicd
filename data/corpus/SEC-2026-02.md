This document describes containment procedures for confirmed breaches. Isolate affected systems from the network before starting forensics.

Escalate confirmed breaches to the CISO within one hour of confirmation. The on-call response lead coordinates containment steps with infrastructure teams.

Forensic images are taken before remediation. Evidence is stored in the restricted evidence vault. Access to this document is limited to the Security department.
