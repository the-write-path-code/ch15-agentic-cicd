All business expenses must be recorded through the expense system. Expense reports are due within 30 days of the end of each month in which the expense was incurred. Late submissions require director approval before reimbursement.

Purchases under $500 need manager approval. Purchases of $500 or more require director approval before the order is placed. The standard per diem for domestic travel is $75 per day.

Receipts are required for any expense above $25. Missing receipts must be explained directly on the report. Approved amounts are paid in the next payroll cycle.
