All remote work requests must be approved in writing by the Chief Operating Officer before any remote access is granted. Department-level arrangements do not replace this approval.

Employees connecting from home must use the company VPN and an enrolled device. Personal devices may not store company data.

Remote sessions are logged and reviewed by the security team. Unapproved remote access is treated as a security incident.
