Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security desk within 24 hours of discovery. Reports can be filed by phone, email, or the incident portal.

Incidents are classified into severity levels 1 through 4. Severity 1 incidents require immediate escalation to the Chief Information Security Officer.

Do not attempt to investigate or contain an incident before the security desk responds. Preserve logs and any related messages for the response team.
